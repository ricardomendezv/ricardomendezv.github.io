02 / Mobile & desktop

KiBoard.

Your PC.
One tap away.

A shortcut deck that connects an Android phone or tablet to a Windows PC. App-aware controls, custom macros, a launcher, and OBS actions over the local network.

Google Play, end to end. Managed the Android app's full Play Console workflow, from store assets and policy declarations to testing and release management.

  • Flutter / Dart
  • Tauri / Rust
  • Svelte
Product & engineering +

The product combines a Flutter mobile app with a Windows companion built with Tauri, Rust, and Svelte. A shared protocol connects device discovery, pairing, live layouts, and actions.

Automatic profiles follow the foreground PC app; a visual editor supports custom decks and multi-step macros. The Android app requires the Windows 11 x64 companion and both devices on the same local network.
